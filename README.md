# 📱 IBUDGETER

Smart Budget Tracker with Barcode Scanner

---

## About

IBUDGETER is a web-based grocery budget tracker that lets you scan product barcodes using your phone's camera. It automatically looks up product information and shows estimated prices based on a built-in SRP database.

---

## Features

- 📷 Barcode scanning via camera
- 💰 Budget tracking with progress bar
- 🛍️ Shopping list with quantity control
- 📊 Real-time spending updates
- 💾 Auto-save to LocalStorage
- 🔍 Manual barcode input fallback
- 🌐 Open Food Facts API integration

---

## Live Demo

👉 https://github.com/ibudgeterresearch-bit/iBUDGETER.

## How to Use

1. **Set your budget** - Enter your total budget and click "Set Budget"
2. **Scan a product** - Point your camera at a barcode
3. **Add to list** - Select quantity and click "Add to List"
4. **Track spending** - Watch your budget update in real-time
